# Developer Certificate of Origin

Contributions to this repository are accepted under the Developer Certificate
of Origin, version 1.1. There is no Contributor License Agreement. Your
contribution is licensed to the project under the same terms as the repository
itself, which are in the LICENSE file.

Sign off each commit with `git commit -s`. That appends a line in this form:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

Use your real name and an email address associated with your account. A
provider-supplied no-reply address is acceptable if you prefer not to publish a
personal one.

## Plain-language summary, not the certificate

This paragraph is explanation only. Where it differs from the text above, the
text above governs. In short: you wrote the contribution, or it came from work
you have the right to pass on under a compatible license, or someone who
certified the same thing handed it to you unchanged. You also understand the
contribution and your sign-off become a permanent public record.
